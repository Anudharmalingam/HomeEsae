🚀 Improvements over Urban Company — build all of these
1. Live GPS tracking — Show worker's real-time location on a map moving toward the user's home. ETA countdown updates every 30 seconds. Use Google Maps API or Leaflet.js. Urban Company only shows a static text status.
2. AI problem diagnosis chatbot — User describes their issue in voice or text ("my tap is leaking and making noise"). AI (Claude API) identifies the exact service needed, gives an estimated price range, and suggests the 3 best available workers nearby. Build this as a floating chat widget on the homepage.
3. Before/after photo proof system — Worker must upload a before photo when starting a job and an after photo when completing it. User reviews photos before approving job completion and releasing payment. Eliminates all disputes. Store photos in Cloudinary or AWS S3.
4. 3-level worker verification badge system — Level 1: ID verified (Aadhaar/PAN). Level 2: Skill test passed (in-app MCQ test per service category). Level 3: Background check cleared. Show all 3 badges on worker profile. Far more trust than a simple star rating.
5. Subscription / membership plan (HomeMaid Plus) — ₹999/month plan includes: 4 cleaning sessions + 1 appliance check + 10% off all bookings + priority support. Build subscription management with auto-renewal. This is your guaranteed monthly recurring revenue.
6. Society / apartment bulk plan — Sell to entire apartment complexes and housing societies. Residents get a group discount (15–20%), workers get guaranteed daily assignments, admin gets a society dashboard. This is a B2B angle Urban Company completely ignores.
7. WhatsApp booking bot — User can book, track, and pay entirely through WhatsApp using Twilio WhatsApp API. Send OTP, booking confirmation, worker details, and payment link all via WhatsApp. Critical for tier-2 and tier-3 cities where users won't install a new app.
8. Worker welfare fund — Automatically deduct 1% from every booking and put it into a worker health/accident fund. Workers can claim from this fund for medical emergencies. Use this as a marketing differentiator — "We care for the people who care for your home."
